Know what you're recycling

What's hiding inside e-waste?

Some electronic products contain substances that can harm people and the environment when handled or disposed of incorrectly.

⚠️

Lead (Pb)

Lead can be present in older screens and circuit boards. If it escapes into soil or water, it can affect the nervous system, blood and kidneys.

Often found in: older displays, circuit boards
☣️

Mercury (Hg)

Some older display lamps and electronic components contain mercury. It can enter the environment and build up through the food chain.

Often found in: some older display lamps
🔋

Cadmium (Cd)

Cadmium has been used in certain rechargeable batteries. Exposure can damage the kidneys and bones, and it is a known carcinogen.

Often found in: some rechargeable batteries
⚠️ Do not dismantle, burn, crush or put electronic waste in household bins. Keep damaged batteries separate and use an authorised e-waste collection or recycling service.
A few helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

What counts as e-waste?

E-waste includes discarded electrical and electronic products such as phones, computers, keyboards, chargers, cables and batteries.

Why shouldn't I throw electronics in regular garbage?

Some electronic components contain hazardous substances. Improper disposal can allow these materials to contaminate soil and water.

How are eco-points calculated?

Each selected category has a fixed demo point value. Your total updates automatically as you change item quantities.

Is this a real pickup service?

No. This website is a frontend project demonstration. Booking details are stored only in your browser and are not sent to a collection agency.
